# Astral Veil

**Astral Veil** is an interactive tarot reading experience built as both a web application and a living world. It blends tarot, zodiac-based readers, celestial themes, hidden lore, and atmospheric storytelling into a site that feels less like a standard reading tool and more like entering a mysterious archive.

Users can choose a Veilwalker, select a tarot spread, reveal cards, explore deck imagery, and uncover deeper story elements tied to the Veil, the Blood Moon, and the hidden truths beneath the surface.

---

## Live Site

Users can view the live site here:

**https://astralveil.world**

---

## Overview

Astral Veil began as a tarot reading project, but has grown into a larger creative world. The site is designed around three core goals:

- Deliver a smooth and intuitive tarot reading experience
- Create a strong mystical atmosphere through visuals, themes, and character design
- Build a deeper lore system where users can discover secrets over time

The project combines front-end development, UI/UX design, interactive storytelling, and worldbuilding.

---

## Features

### Tarot Readings

Users can choose a reader and select from available tarot spreads:

- 3-card reading
- 5-card reading
- 7-card reading

Each reading reveals card meanings, upright and shadow interpretations, reflection prompts, and a final combined section called **The Thread Between the Cards**.

---

### Veilwalkers

The Veilwalkers are zodiac-inspired readers connected to the Astral Veil. Each one has their own visual identity, element, tone, and reading style.

Users can browse the Veilwalkers, view their details, and choose who will guide their reading.

---

### Theme Modes

Astral Veil includes multiple visual modes:

- **Sun Mode**: warmer, clearer, and softer
- **Moon Mode**: mystical, calm, and celestial
- **Blood Moon Mode**: darker, secretive, and confrontational

The Blood Moon mode is not just a color change. It is designed as a hidden layer of the site, revealing deeper lore, altered language, unique visuals, and a more intense shadow-work tone.

---

### Deck Viewer

Astral Veil includes a tarot deck viewing experience where users can explore card artwork and descriptions.

Current decks include:

- **Original Deck**
- **Blood Moon Deck**

The Blood Moon Deck is designed as a darker alternate deck connected to the secret Blood Moon event.

---

### Noctis Archive

The **Noctis Archive** is a lore-focused section of Astral Veil that stores recovered entries, research, visual records, and restricted fragments.

Current archive sections include:

- Zephyra’s Journal
- Research
- Visual Records
- Restricted Fragments

This section is intended to grow into a deeper mystery system where hidden fragments and story pieces can be uncovered over time.

## Planned Features

Astral Veil is still evolving. Planned or experimental ideas include:

- Unlockable hidden fragments
- A 13-fragment collection system
- Puzzle-based lore discoveries
- Cipher/code-based secrets
- Expanded Noctis Archive entries
- Additional Blood Moon content
- More character phases and transformations
- A future account system to save user progress across devices

## Design Philosophy

Astral Veil is designed around the idea that:

> The world should feel mysterious, but the interface should remain clear.

The project aims to avoid clutter while still feeling immersive. The layout is built so users can easily understand what to do next, while the atmosphere, characters, and hidden lore create the feeling of entering another world.

The site takes inspiration from:

- Tarot and occult symbolism
- Zodiac archetypes
- Celestial fantasy
- Gothic atmosphere
- Interactive fiction
- Video game menus and character selection screens
- Mystery archives and hidden lore systems

## Testing Hooks

- Add `?bloodMoon=1` or `?testBloodMoon=1` to any page URL to activate the Blood Moon event for the current browser session.
- Add `?bloodMoon=0` to clear the Blood Moon event.

## Publishing Notes

All paths are relative and domain-ready for static hosting. Large card and reader images should be compressed before production deployment for faster first load.
