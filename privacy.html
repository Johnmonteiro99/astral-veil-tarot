Astral Veil

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 25, 2026
Last Updated: June 25, 2026

This page describes what Astral Veil may collect, store, and use when you create an account, save content, journal, interact with archive or gallery features, or use the site.

Account Data We May Collect

  • Email address and account sign-in details.
  • Display name, birthday month/day, and preferences if you choose to provide them.
  • Saved readings, journal entries, archive progress, reader or deck preferences, unlock progress, and account settings.
  • Gallery activity tied to your account, such as saved, marked, recovered, or recently viewed records.
  • Technical or usage data such as device or browser information, error logs, and basic site activity used to improve and protect the site.
  • Cookies, local storage, or similar technologies used to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and improve the experience.

How Data Is Used

  • Provide account access.
  • Save and restore your content.
  • Personalize reading, archive, gallery, and journal experiences.
  • Remember preferences.
  • Protect the site from abuse or unauthorized access.
  • Debug, maintain, and improve Astral Veil.
  • Communicate account-related notices if needed.

Private Account Content

Journal entries are private to your user account and are not publicly visible. Astral Veil does not intentionally publish or share journal entries.

Journal entries may be processed only as needed to provide, secure, debug, maintain, or legally operate the service. Saved readings and account activity are also tied to your account so they can be restored when you sign in.

Service Providers

Astral Veil may use trusted service providers for hosting, authentication, database storage, analytics, email delivery, payment processing, security, or similar operations. These providers may process data only as needed to provide their services.

Data Sharing

Astral Veil does not sell your account data. Data may be shared only with service providers, when required by law, to protect Astral Veil or users, or with your consent.

Security

Astral Veil uses reasonable safeguards to protect account data. No online service can guarantee perfect security.

Data Deletion and Retention

You may request account deletion from Account Settings or by contacting privacy@astralveil.world. Astral Veil will delete or anonymize account-related data where reasonably possible.

Some limited records may be retained for security, legal, fraud prevention, backup, or technical reasons.

Your Choices

  • You may update certain profile details.
  • You may delete journal entries where supported.
  • You may clear supported account activity, including some Gallery activity through Privacy Controls.
  • You may request account deletion.
  • You may choose what optional details you add to your account.

International Users

If you access Astral Veil from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the country where Astral Veil or its service providers operate.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated as Astral Veil grows. Updated policy text will be posted on this page.

Contact

Signed-in users can contact the Astral Veil team from Account Settings. General support is available at support@astralveil.world. Privacy and account deletion requests can be sent to privacy@astralveil.world.
