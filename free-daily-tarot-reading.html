Tarot Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the daily tarot reading free?

Yes. You can start a free daily tarot reading on Astral Veil and draw a card for reflection, clarity, and emotional grounding.

Do I need an account?

No account is required to begin a reading. Signing in unlocks additional features such as saving readings and journaling afterward.

Is this meant to predict the future?

No. Astral Veil readings are designed for reflection, self-awareness, and emotional insight. They are not meant to offer certainty or replace professional advice.

Can I save or journal about my reading?

Yes, if you are signed in. Astral Veil allows you to return to your reading and use journaling features to reflect more deeply afterward.

How often can I do a daily reading?

You can return each day for a new daily reading. The practice is meant to be simple, steady, and reflective rather than overwhelming.

Do I need to know tarot to use Astral Veil?

Not at all. Astral Veil is designed to feel welcoming whether you are new to tarot or already familiar with it.

How do I choose a Veilwalker?

Choose the Veilwalker whose energy, style, or zodiac presence feels most aligned with what you want to explore. You do not need a perfect reason. Intuition is enough.

What makes Astral Veil different from other tarot sites?

Astral Veil is more than a reading tool. It combines tarot, reflection, immersive storytelling, curated decks, and a mystical world designed to support self-discovery.
