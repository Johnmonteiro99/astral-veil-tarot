Frequently Asked Questions

What is an online tarot reading?

An online tarot reading lets you draw tarot cards digitally and use their meanings as symbolic guidance for reflection, clarity, and self-discovery.

How does Astral Veil's online tarot reading work?

Choose a Veilwalker, select a deck, choose a spread, reveal your cards, and read the interpretations as reflective guidance.

Is this online tarot reading free?

Yes. You can begin a free online tarot reading with Astral Veil. Some extras, such as saving readings or journaling, may require an account.

Do I need an account to start?

No account is required to start a reading. Signing in unlocks extras such as saved readings and journaling afterward.

Can I choose my reader and deck?

Yes. Astral Veil lets you choose a reader and deck before selecting the spread for your online tarot card reading.

Can I save or journal about my reading?

Yes, if you are signed in. You can save what matters and use the journal to continue reflecting after the reading.

Is tarot meant to predict the future?

Astral Veil treats tarot as symbolic guidance for reflection. Readings are not guaranteed predictions and do not replace professional advice.
