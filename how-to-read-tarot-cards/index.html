      Foundations · Set the Intention

      01

      Prepare the Reading

      You do not need psychic abilities to read tarot. You need attention, curiosity, and a question that leaves room for choice.

      Build a clear beginning

      Choose a deck you can see clearly, make a calm space, keep a journal nearby, and decide on a one- or three-card spread before shuffling.

      1. Settle your attentionTake a few slow breaths and let the pace soften.
      2. Name the reading’s purposeBe clear about what you are seeking.
      3. Choose a simple spreadStart with one or three cards.
      4. Record the questionWrite it down before you shuffle.
      At this level

      Start with one narrow question and one to three cards. Let your first impression arrive before checking a guidebook.

      Deepen the ritual

      Establish a consistent opening routine, decide whether you will use reversals, and note your emotional expectations before drawing.

      Define the framework

      Record assumptions, reader bias, ethical boundaries, and whether the reading is reflective, predictive, or decision-supportive before interpreting.

      Foundations · Create Useful Scope

      02

      Form the Question

      Useful questions are open-ended, specific enough to focus the spread, and centered on your own choices.

      Move from certainty to agency

      Questions become clearer when they ask what you can understand, notice, or choose.

      Unclear version

      “Will this definitely happen?”

      Clearer direction

      Ask what you can understand, notice, or choose.

      Question Workshop

      A clearer question

      What should I understand about the direction of this situation?

      Choose your intent
      Focus

      Use “what,” “how,” or “where can I focus” to create room for insight and action.

      Compare the emphasis

      “What supports this relationship?” invites resources; “What challenges it?” directs attention toward friction. Wording changes the lens.

      Interrogate the frame

      Notice hidden assumptions, excluded perspectives, emotional stakes, and whether a different question would better preserve agency.

      Foundations · See Before You Define

      03

      Read the Imagery

      Soften your gaze. Notice the figure, gesture, setting, color, objects, direction of movement, and the feeling the scene creates.

      Describe before interpreting

      Choose one layer at a time. Describe what you can see before deciding what it means. Your emotional response matters, but it is not proof.

      Work from visible evidence toward a possible interpretation, then leave room for another reading.

      Figure

      Begin with the central presence

      Notice who occupies the scene, how they are dressed, and what role they seem to hold before naming an archetype.

      Practice

      Name three visible details, then turn them into one plain sentence about the scene.

      Read relationships

      Compare foreground and background, visual contrast, repeated motifs, and the direction one symbol points toward another.

      Hold ambiguity

      Consider historical symbolism, deck-specific reinterpretation, composition, and contradictory visual cues without forcing one final answer.

      Interpretation · The Deck’s Grammar

      04

      Understand Suit + Number

      Major Arcana cards often emphasize large turning points. Minor Arcana cards show daily experience through a suit, a number or court rank, and the card’s position.

      Compose the layers

      Read the suit as the realm of experience and the number as the stage of movement. Combine both with the question and spread position.

      Study the Major ArcanaUnderstand the Minor Arcana and Four Suits

      Ace + WandsA spark of action, creativity, or desire beginning to take form.

      Build a sentence

      Read the suit as the realm and the number as the stage: a Three of Cups can suggest emotion or relationship growing through connection.

      Combine the layers

      Connect suit, number, court rank, and position. Ask which layer is strongest in the context of the question.

      Compare the system

      Read elemental affinity, imbalance, absent suits, court dynamics, and differences between numerological traditions.

      Interpretation · Meaning in Context

      05

      Interpret the Position

      A card does not change identity when its position changes, but the position decides which part of the question it addresses.

      Let the position assign the role

      Select any position by touch, mouse, or keyboard. Then complete the sentence “In this position, The Star suggests…”

      Drag-and-drop is optional; every meaning remains available through the controls.

      The Star · Past

      Hope that shaped the present

      A period of healing, renewed trust, or clearer purpose may be an important foundation for the current situation.

      Use the role directly

      Complete this sentence: “In the position of Past, The Star suggests…”

      Modify the base meaning

      Ask how the role narrows, redirects, or complicates the card’s familiar keywords.

      Allow parallel readings

      Compare which plausible reading is better supported by the question and surrounding cards.

      Interpretation · Direction and Expression

      06

      Read Upright + Reversed

      A shift in direction can shift the deeper meaning. Reversals are optional and are not automatically negative.

      Choose a consistent framework

      Upright can describe direct or available expression. Reversed can suggest energy that is blocked, delayed, private, internalized, or overextended.

      If reversals make the reading less clear, read every card upright while you build confidence.

      Upright expression

      Directed outward

      • Focused skill
      • Available resources
      • Clear intention
      Activate the card to rotate
      Reversed expression

      Redirected inward

      • Blocked or delayed
      • Private or underused
      • Scattered intention
      The Magician

      Focused expression

      Skill, attention, and available resources are moving directly toward an intention.

      Keep it simple

      If reversals make the reading less clear, read every card upright while you build confidence.

      Compare expression

      Ask whether the card’s energy is direct, blocked, private, delayed, or overextended in relation to nearby cards.

      Choose a framework

      Compare visual inversion, elemental dignity, shadow expression, and contextual modification—then use one framework consistently.

      Interpretation · From Cards to Story

      07

      Connect the Cards

      After reading each card in position, zoom out. Repetition, concentration, direction, contradiction, and absence create a second layer of meaning.

      Read the spread as a relationship

      The featured Three of Cups and Three of Wands repeat the number Three across emotional and active realms. The cards between them change how that growth unfolds.

      After noticing individual meanings, summarize the full pattern in one sentence.

      Repetition

      A shared number carries weight

      The two Threes echo growth and development across emotional and active realms.

      One-sentence method

      Read every card, then summarize the whole spread in one sentence beginning, “This reading is about…”

      Pattern method

      Rank the two strongest patterns and explain how they reinforce or challenge one another.

      Synthesis method

      Interpret harmony, contradiction, missing elements, sequence, and competing narratives while distinguishing evidence from reader projection.

      Integration · Close the Reading

      08

      Reflect + Integrate

      End by naming what the reading helped you notice—not what it proved. Record the cards, question, first impression, and one grounded next step.

      What changed in my understanding, and what choice remains mine?

      What image, phrase, or feeling continues to hold your attention?

      This stays only in the current field and is not saved or transmitted automatically.
